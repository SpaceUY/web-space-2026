The blockchain community keeps growing with fresh events emerging worldwide. These gatherings—ranging from large conferences to smaller meetups—welcome developers, investors, and enthusiasts to network, learn, and collaborate. This comprehensive guide highlights upcoming Ethereum events through year-end.

"If you're looking to deepen your understanding of blockchain technology, connect with industry peers, or discover the latest advancements in the ecosystem, these events are not to be missed."

## Events Agenda

**🇦🇷 Ethereum Argentina (ETH Argentina): Aug 2, 2024**
- Workshop week: July 29–August 1
- Conference: August 2
- Hackathon: August 2–4
- Location: Golden Center, Buenos Aires
- Website: https://ethereumargentina.org/

**🇧🇴 Ethereum Bolivia (ETH Bolivia): Aug 9, 2024**
- Buildathon featuring developer collaboration
- Seminars, workshops, talks, and competitions
- Location: Universidad Privada del Valle, Santa Cruz
- Website: https://www.ethereumbolivia.org/buildathon-2024

**🇨🇦 Ethereum Toronto (ETH Toronto): Aug 13, 2024**
- Part of Blockchain Futurist Conference
- Workshops, talks, networking opportunities
- Location: Rebel Entertainment Complex, Toronto
- Website: https://www.ethtoronto.ca/

**🇨🇦 Ethereum Women (ETHWomen): Aug 13, 2024**
- Female-focused hackathon in Toronto
- Mentorship and diversity-promoting activities
- Online and in-person participation
- Website: https://www.ethwomen.com/

**🇺🇾 Ethereum Uruguay (ETH Uruguay): Aug 16, 2024**
- Buildathon emphasizing innovation and community engagement
- Workshops, talks, collaborative activities
- Location: Sinergia Faro, Montevideo
- Website: https://www.ethereumuruguay.org/

**🇯🇵 Ethereum Tokyo (ETH Tokyo): Aug 23, 2024**
- Tracks: freedom of transacting, robust democracy, infrastructure/tooling
- Hacking, workshops, networking
- Location: Digital Garage, Tokyo
- Website: https://www.ethtokyo.com/

**🌐 ETHOnline (ETH Global): Aug 23, 2024**
- Asynchronous hackathon with $225,000 in prizes
- Pre-event workshops and team formation sessions
- Worldwide participation
- Website: https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2024

**🇵🇱 Ethereum Warsaw (ETH Warsaw): Sep 5, 2024**
- Approximately 1,000 participants expected
- Topics: privacy, security, scalability beyond Layer 2
- Workshops, talks, hackathons
- Location: Browary Warszawskie, Warsaw
- Website: https://www.ethwarsaw.dev/

**🇮🇹 Ethereum Napul (Napul ETH): Sep 12, 2024**
- Southern Italy's largest web3 event
- Tracks: education, art/NFT, law, DeFi, gaming
- Cultural experiences and networking
- Location: Monumental Complex of Santa Maria la Nova, Naples
- Website: https://napul.eth.limo/

**🇲🇽 Ethereum Mexico (Mexico ETH): Sep 13, 2024**
- Hackathons, workshops, talks from industry leaders
- Location: Centro Internacional de Congresos de Yucatán, Mérida
- Website: https://ethmexico.org/

**🇮🇹 Ethereum Milan (ETH Milan): Sep 26, 2024**
- Three main tracks: "Crea" (smart contracts/DeFi), "Ispira" (art/design intersection)
- Creative hackathon, panel discussions, keynotes
- Location: Teatro Franco Parenti, Milan
- Website: https://www.ethmilan.xyz/

**🇮🇹 Ethereum Rome (ETH Rome): Oct 4, 2024**
- Focus: account abstraction and privacy challenges
- Cultural experiences and networking side events
- Location: Via Beata Maria de Mattias, Rome
- Website: https://www.ethrome.org/

**🇲🇾 Ethereum Kuala Lumpur (ETHKL): Oct 4, 2024**
- Talks and hackathons
- Open, equitable blockchain focus
- Workshops and networking activities
- Location: Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre
- Website: https://www.2024.ethkl.org/

**🇧🇬 ETH Sofia: Oct 17, 2024**
- Over 40 speakers
- Tracks: DeFi, ZK proofs, AI, Layer 2, UX, infrastructure

- Location: Innovation Forum John Atanasoff, Sofia Tech Park
- Website: https://www.ethsofia.com/

- Location: Palace of Fine Arts, San Francisco
