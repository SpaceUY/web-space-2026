## The Challenge

At its core, FightFI aims to boost user retention and incentivize activity by merging classic gaming elements with modern Web3 infrastructure. Users connect their wallets, complete on-chain actions, and compete for prizes, all while interacting with a product that evolves in real time. Pulling that off meant building a polished consumer experience on top of unfamiliar blockchain primitives, without compromising on either side.

## Our Approach

Our team partnered closely with FightFI from the ground up, helping define the product vision, design its architecture, and deliver both frontend and backend components. From smart contract deployment to UI enhancements and admin tooling, we've supported the project across multiple layers of development and continue to iterate as the product grows.

## Results

The platform now ships a daily-engagement loop powered by NFTs and on-chain claims, supported by an internal admin tool the FightFI team uses to operate the product end-to-end. The architecture is ready for the next phase: token integration and deeper on-chain mechanics.
